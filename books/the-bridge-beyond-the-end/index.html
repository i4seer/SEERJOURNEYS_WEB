Inside the story

ALDER • BEYOND MILE 24

THE BRIDGE IS STILL STANDING.

THE ROAD ACROSS IT IS NOT.

THE FAR SIDE HOLDS A NAME.

Eleven-year-old Sam Calder has earned a planned trip beyond Mile 24. Past the stone shelter, he and Mara find a steel bridge spanning a gorge on a road Alder insists goes nowhere.

The trusses still stand. Part of the timber deck does not. Sam sees a difficult crossing and a chance to prove he belongs there. Mara sees a bad plan—and a safer maintenance route descending beneath the bridge.

Across the water, repair plates reveal decades of hidden work and a corroded sign gives the forgotten road a name: NORTH LINK. But reaching the far side means nothing if they cannot get home on time.

A good plan includes the way home.
