Inside the story

FENWICK • TWO MILES NORTH

THE MAP SHOWS EMPTY COUNTRY.

THE ROAD SHOWS FRESH TRACKS.

FENWICK IS VERY MUCH ALIVE.

Twelve-year-old Sam Calder and Mara set out on their first overnight journey expecting ruins beyond the North Link bridge. Fresh tire tracks, a repaired fence, and woodsmoke lead instead to Fenwick—a living town Alder's maps forgot.

Fenwick has homes, trucks, homework, and its own story of why the road closed. Alder says the northern route failed. Fenwick says Alder shut it down. The surviving records say both towns simplified the past.

Then a storm tears out the way home. To cross South Fork, the children and Fenwick's road crew must build from both banks. By the time the boards hold, two separated towns have each other's phone numbers—and no way to become strangers again.

A town can vanish from a map without going anywhere.

Sam’s progression

Sam stops treating Alder’s map as the default truth and begins comparing records from both sides of the road.
