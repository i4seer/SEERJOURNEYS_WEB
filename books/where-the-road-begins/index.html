Inside the story

ZERO HOUSE • THE FINAL MEETING

THE MAP IS COMPLETE.

THE WEATHER IS CLOSING IN.

THE DECISION BELONGS TO EVERYONE.

Nineteen-year-old Sam Calder has spent ten years finding the roads Alder forgot. Now representatives from every surviving community are traveling to Zero House to decide what those roads become—and a hard seasonal front is closing the safest approaches.

Before anyone can argue about the future, they must get there. A temporary span across the Kerr River needs two banks to own its inspections. Snow closes the high route. Every changed plan forces towns that disagree about the road to depend on one another.

Sam has the complete map and the evidence to release it. He no longer believes that makes the decision his. The new compact must say which routes are shared, which remain closed, who maintains them, and when everyone meets again.

A road begins when people meet it from both sides.

Sam’s progression

Sam’s final progression is from discovering roads to helping build systems that do not depend on him.
