The series

One boy. One road. Ten years of learning what a map can’t tell you.

JOURNEY is a grounded middle-grade adventure series about curiosity growing into responsibility. Every book covers one year of Sam’s life. Every journey adds real geography, real people, and a new kind of problem to the same expanding network. Book 1 is published now; the rest are on the way.

The early books ask whether the road is there and how far Sam can safely follow it. The later books ask harder questions: Which histories can coexist? Who gets to publish a route? Who maintains a bridge after the exciting part is over?

The larger idea

A road does not stay connected because somebody found it.

It stays connected because people inspect it, close it when conditions change, repair it, argue about it, share responsibility, and come back later to review the choices they made.
