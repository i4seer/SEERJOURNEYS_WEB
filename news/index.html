News

Series, map, and publication updates for Journey.

September 2026

Books 1–3 are published.

The series now has three books available in paperback and ebook: Mile Twenty-Four, The Bridge Beyond the End, and The Town Off the Map. Together they cover Sam Calder from age ten to twelve, from his first walk past Mile 23 to the discovery of Fenwick. Buy on Amazon: Book 1 · Book 2 · Book 3

September 2026

New front covers for the full series.

Cover art is now finished for all ten books and is shown across this site, from the home page through every book page.

September 2026

Where the rest of the series stands.

Books 4 through 10 are fully outlined, with one book planned for each year of Sam’s journey from age thirteen to nineteen.

September 2026

The reader map grows book by book.

This site includes a progressive map for every book and a full-series schematic. The maps preserve the distinction between historic, current, seasonal, broken, and controlled-access routes.
